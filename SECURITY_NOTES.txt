PRIVATE CHARACTER CHAT 5.0 — SECURITY AND PRIVACY

The original encrypted chat store is preserved. New social text and metadata use
Android Keystore AES-GCM with AtomicFile write/rollback. Corrupt reads preserve
stored data instead of silently replacing it. These Android storage paths have
not been exercised on a physical device in this session.

Imported JPEGs are app-private, but are not individually AES-encrypted. Imports
normalize orientation and re-encode images (not a comprehensive privacy audit).
Public comment prompts do not include private DM history. Social characters are
fictional and local, not real people or a real Instagram account.

Photo sharing is request-only by default. Public automatic attachments require
separate opt-in. Captions/tags/descriptions come from the user: the text model
cannot inspect image pixels or validate their contents.

The original INTERNET permission remains for model downloads. App data backups
remain disabled in the manifest/extraction rules. A rooted/compromised/unlocked
phone can expose data. Notification previews depend on phone settings.

The native llama-android AAR is third-party and unaudited here. LLM output is not
reliable real-world advice. Application passwords/signing keystore already present
in the supplied source are retained: do not publish this project publicly or reuse
this development signing identity for a public production distribution.

No APK or APK signature was produced or tested this session. Keystore inspection
is only evidence that the existing signing key has been preserved.
